📖 Quick Guide
  1. Export your Google Form responses as CSV or XLSX.
  2. Upload the file here — original row order is preserved (= timestamp order).
  3. Map the columns: choose Name, Email, Department, custom ID prefix, and extra columns.
  4. Download the QR ZIP and distribute QR passes to participants.
  5. On event day, volunteers open the AttendQR Mobile App / Scanner with the Event Code to scan attendees in simultaneously.
  6. Track real-time multi-device attendance on the Dashboard and export final CSVs.
